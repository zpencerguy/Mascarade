## Setup Notes

- Each player starts with 6 gold coins.
- The remaining money forms the bank.
- The courthouse is placed away from the bank.
- Character cards are shuffled and each player receives one, face up.
- After all players study the cards, they are turned face down.
- Character tokens track which characters are present.
- The youngest player begins, then play proceeds clockwise.

## Character Choice Restrictions

- The Judge must be in play.
- At least one-third of the characters, if not more, should bring money from
  the bank: Queen, King, Widow, Fool, and the Peasants.
- Both Peasant cards must always be included together.
- Peasants and Inquisitor require at least 8 players if included.
- In games with more than 5 players, one or two middle cards may optionally be
  added, like in 4 and 5 player games.
